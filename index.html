Qi Wang (王祺)

Lecturer, North China Electric Power University (NCEPU)

Currently, I am a lecturer at North China Electric Power University (NCEPU). Earlier, I received my Ph.D. degree at State Key Lab of CAD&CG, Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Hujun Bao and Prof. Yuchi Huo. Previously, I received my BS and MS degrees from the Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT).

My research interests lie in realistic rendering, inverse rendering, 3D reconstruction, and computer vision.
